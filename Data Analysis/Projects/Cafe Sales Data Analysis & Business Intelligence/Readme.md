<h1>Cafe Sales Data Analysis & Business Intelligence</h1>

<h2>Project Overview</h2>
<p>An end-to-end Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on daily transaction records to transform raw sales data into actionable business intelligence.</p>

<hr>

<h2>Technical Stack</h2>
<ul>
  <li><b>Language:</b> Python</li>
  <li><b>Data Processing:</b> Pandas, NumPy</li>
  <li><b>Visualization:</b> Matplotlib, Seaborn</li>
  <li><b>Environment:</b> Jupyter Notebook</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2>Data Engineering & Optimization</h2>
<ul>
  <li><b>Anomaly Handling:</b> Converted invalid text strings to standard datetime formats.</li>
  <li><b>Missing Values:</b> Imputed categorical missing data without row loss.</li>
  <li><b>Memory Optimization:</b> Cast low-cardinality text columns to <code>category</code> types.</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2>Key Business Insights</h2>

<h3>1. Volume vs. Revenue Dynamics</h3>
<ul>
  <li><b>Observation:</b> Coffee drives transaction volume; Salads generate the highest overall revenue.</li>
  <li><b>Recommendation:</b> Use high-volume drinks as foot-traffic drivers to cross-sell high-margin food.</li>
</ul>

<h3>2. Operational Logistics</h3>
<ul>
  <li><b>Observation:</b> Beverages/baked goods lean heavily toward Takeaway, while full meals dominate In-Store.</li>
</ul>
